# National Backlog Australia

### A country's issue register.

National Backlog Australia is an open, evidence-driven initiative for tracking unresolved national challenges, emerging risks, and measurable progress over time.

Every successful organisation maintains a backlog, risk register, performance indicators, ownership, and continuous improvement processes.

A nation deserves the same level of visibility.

## Our Purpose

We exist to help Australians answer a few simple questions:

- What are our most important unresolved challenges?
- Are they improving or deteriorating?
- How do we know?
- What evidence supports that assessment?
- What risks are emerging?

## Our Principles

### Evidence Before Opinion

Claims should be supported by data, research, official reports, and credible sources.

### No Blame. Only Ownership.

We do not assign blame.

We focus on visibility, accountability, progress, and outcomes.

### Measure Outcomes

Success is not measured by announcements.

Success is measured by real-world improvements.

### Preserve Institutional Memory

Important challenges should not disappear from public awareness because a new headline emerges.

### Use Technology to Improve Visibility

Data, automation, artificial intelligence, and open collaboration can help societies better understand risks, progress, and long-term trends.
